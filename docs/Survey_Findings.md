We conducted interviews with students who have lived away from their hometowns during their engineering studies. We found that the major problems aren't limited to finding accommodation. Students reported conflicts related to cleanliness, noise, sleep schedules, food preferences, personal-space habits, and study patterns. We also found issues with transportation, night-time safety, broker-owner-student miscommunication, and discovering useful local shops and food places.

Based on these findings, we want to refine StayMatch so that the roommate matching isn't based only on basic profile information. The LCI will consider the actual day-to-day living factors students identified through our survey.

| Category                  | Problems students reported                                      |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🏠 Accommodation          | Finding suitable accommodation, misleading information          |
| 🤝 Roommate compatibility | Different mindsets, habits, study fields, native places         |
| 🧹 Cleanliness            | Different standards of cleanliness                              |
| 🔊 Noise                  | Roommate talking on phone at night, different noise tolerance   |
| 💤 Sleep habits           | Early sleeper vs late-night roommate                            |
| 🌬️ Personal habits        | Window/balcony open vs closed preferences                       |
| 🍛 Food                   | Jain / vegetarian / non-vegetarian / different food preferences |
| 🍽️ Food availability      | Difficulty finding good nearby food                             |
| 🛒 Local services         | Good grocery/shops/restaurants not easily discoverable online   |
| 🚗 Transportation         | Difficulty finding convenient transport                         |
| 🌙 Safety                 | Feeling unsafe at night                                         |
| 💰 Rent transparency      | Broker/owner miscommunication and changing rent amounts         |
| 🏠 Location               | Finding accommodation near useful facilities                    |
| 👥 Social compatibility   | Preference for people from same/similar background or mindset   |
| 🎓 Academic compatibility | Preference for same department/study field                      |
